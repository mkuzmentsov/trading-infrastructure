— SIZING RULE —
• COMPOUND (reinvest, let it grow): cap leverage at 2× — the highest leverage with 0%
  liquidation AND positive ln-growth (g>0). Above 2×, g=−∞: repeated betting is
  mathematically certain ruin no matter how good the signal. This is the 'don't roll it' wall.
• LOTTERY (high leverage, ≥5–10×): typical outcome is $0 (median≈0, P(total loss) high). Size it
  ONLY as fully-losable capital — the $3,000 stake here, lost 100% with no effect on the
  $97,000 core. NEVER size by the mean/95th: those are the survivorship
  moonshots the leaderboard shows you. Pick leverage by the payoff distribution you accept.
