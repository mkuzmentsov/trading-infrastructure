Mechanism (per-regime breakdown, 1h): in TRENDING bars the intraday signal IS positive
(trend_up Sharpe +7.18 / +10.9% ret, trend_down +4.87 / +6.0%), but RANGE bars are 57-61% of all

= more whipsaw = more fee bleed. The DAILY bar averages through intraday noise, so the same trend
signal survives there. Conclusion: the edge is a horizon property; it does not exist intraday.
