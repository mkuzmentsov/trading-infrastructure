## 0. Method — and why this is *not* a replay

### 0.1 The instrument: the fleet's own live fill ledger

The house fill model exists because most questions have no live counterfactual. These four
leads mostly do not need one — they ask *"is cell X still bad in the current fleet?"* — so the
right instrument is the fleet's **actual fills**, not a simulation.

Pulled from `data-api.polymarket.com/activity?user=0xdb66d896…` (the bots' proxy wallet),
joined to `gamma /markets?closed=true` outcome prices. 1,976 BUY prints 08-26 → 09-10;
**1,141 prints / 1,094 bars / 10 days** in the 09-01 → 09-10 window. Fee applied as
`sz × 0.07 × p × (1−p)`.

**Fee model verified exactly against the venue.** For the first row, `size` 24 @ `price` 0.99
gives `usdcSize` 23.77663; `24 × 0.99 = 23.76` and the predicted fee is `24 × 0.07 × 0.99 ×
0.01 = 0.016632` → 23.776632. The venue's own `usdcSize` **includes** the taker fee. The
model is not an approximation.

## Lead 1 — UP/DOWN asymmetry

### Original claim
`analytics-20260830.md` §7, era 08-16 → 08-30, 1,643 settled bars:
UP **+$235.12** (+2.09%, n = 816) vs DOWN **−$148.29** (−1.27%, n = 827).
Proposed knob: a higher est threshold for DOWN clips. The doc itself suspected trend loading;
§58's ship note recorded "UP/DOWN asymmetry NOT deployed (08-27 inversion = trend loading)".

### Pre-registered decision rule
Ship-candidate only if DOWN is negative out of sample, the UP−DOWN bar-mean difference clears
t ≥ 2.5 after multiplicity, **and** it survives day-wise and coin-wise LOO.

### Re-measurement, 09-01 → 09-10, live ledger, bar level

| side | bars | net | $/day | ROI | loss bars | mean/bar | SE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UP | 537 | **+$96.89** | +9.69 | +0.94% | 21 (2.1/day) | 0.180 | 0.225 |
| DOWN | 557 | **+$60.94** | +6.09 | +0.56% | 15 (1.5/day) | 0.109 | 0.139 |

UP − DOWN = **+0.071 $/bar, bootstrap SE 0.270, t = 0.26, p = 0.84.**

**DOWN is positive out of sample.** The claim's headline — DOWN loses money — does not
replicate at all.

### Leave-one-out

| fold | UP net excl. fold | DOWN net excl. fold |
|---|---|---|
| worst day (09-06) | **−$13.51** | (09-03) +$6.01 |
| worst coin (bnb) | **−$31.55** | (hype) +$14.52 |

UP's entire edge is one day (09-06, the +$110 dislocation-windfall day) and one coin (bnb,
+$128 of a +$97 total). Remove either and **UP is negative**. DOWN survives both folds
positive. If anything the LOO evidence runs *backwards* to the claim.

### Sign stability
- UP > 0 on 8/10 days, DOWN > 0 on 6/10 days, but **UP > DOWN on only 5/10 days and 3/7
  coins** — a coin flip.
- Within the sample the sign inverts: 09-03 is DOWN +$54.92 / UP −$36.56; 09-06 is
  UP +$110.40 / DOWN +$12.79.
- Third fold, the 08-26 → 08-31 window (pre/at-§58, 471 bars): UP +$17.81 / DOWN −$41.41 —
  same *sign* as the original claim, opposite sign to the test window. Two adjacent windows
  disagree.

### Mechanism test: is it drift loading?
Classifying each bar by whether its coin's UP-resolution fraction that day was ≥ 0.5:

| | DOWN bets | UP bets |
|---|---|---|
| coin-day drifted **down** | −$21.56 (n 265) | +$78.16 (n 241) |
| coin-day drifted **up** | +$82.49 (n 292) | +$18.73 (n 296) |

The side that pays is the one **against** the day's realised drift, and it flips with the
drift — i.e. the "side" variable is a proxy for regime, exactly as suspected on 08-30. The
against-drift cell itself is **+0.301 vs −0.005 $/bar, diff t = 1.16, p = 0.24** → noise, and
in any case the day's drift sign is only knowable at day end, so it is not an ex-ante knob.
Coin-day correlation of side-net with UP-fraction: 0.066 (UP), 0.178 (DOWN) — nothing.

### Verdict — ⛔ **REFUTED**
Out of sample DOWN makes money, the difference is t = 0.26, and the UP leg is carried by one
day and one coin (the exact failure mode that killed buy-the-dip). The 08-16 → 08-30 table
was regime loading. **Do not build a DOWN-side est premium.** Close permanently.

## Lead 3 — the 04-08 UTC hole

### Original claim
`analytics-20260830.md` §5, era 08-16 → 08-30: 04-08 UTC = **−$68.48 (n = 262)**, the only
strongly negative 4-hour block; 16-24 UTC = +$123. The doc flagged its own risk ("single bars
CAN own a bucket"). §60 (08-30 20:00K) later recorded it as a **3-bar artifact (ex-3
+$18.52)** — but never with LOO on independent data. This is that test.

### Pre-registered decision rule
Ship-candidate only if 04-08 is negative, t ≤ −2.5 after multiplicity, and no single day or
coin owns it.

### Re-measurement, 09-01 → 09-10, live ledger, bar level

| block | bars | net | $/day | ROI | loss bars | mean/bar | t |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 00-04 | 172 | +$103.03 | +10.30 | +3.05% | 4 | 0.599 | 1.19 |
| **04-08** | **164** | **+$4.18** | **+0.42** | **+0.13%** | 4 (0.4/day) | 0.025 | **0.09** |
| 08-12 | 196 | −$67.19 | −6.72 | −1.81% | 9 | −0.343 | −0.96 |
| 12-16 | 231 | +$2.46 | +0.25 | +0.05% | 10 | 0.011 | 0.05 |
| 16-20 | 172 | +$125.37 | +12.54 | +3.68% | 3 | 0.729 | 2.71 |
| 20-24 | 159 | −$10.01 | −1.00 | −0.36% | 6 | −0.063 | −0.27 |

04-08 is **flat, not a hole**: +$4.18, mean 0.025 $/bar, and against a permutation null
drawing 164 bars at random it sits at **z = −0.42, one-sided p = 0.34**. It is positive on
6 of 10 days, and its worst three bars (−$23.85 eth, −$23.79 eth, −$23.78 xrp) are ordinary
full-clip flips, two of which are ≥0.98 entries with nothing time-of-day about them.

### Leave-one-out (on the block, as if it were the candidate cell)
Excluding 09-06 the block is **−$10.01**; excluding 09-09 it is −$7.69; excluding hype it is
−$19.47; excluding sol −$12.79. It flips sign on removal of any of several single days or
coins in *either* direction — the definition of a cell with no signal.

### The block that *is* negative this window, and why it is also nothing
08-12 UTC reads −$67.19, but t = −0.96, and LOO kills it too: excluding bnb it is −$88.73,
excluding sol −$63.99, i.e. it is spread thin rather than owned — but at |t| < 1 against a
null whose 6-block max|t| p95 is 3.66, it is indistinguishable from noise. The strongest
block in the table is **16-20 UTC at t = 2.71**, which is exactly the t ≈ 2-3 range the
structure hunt established as the noise band; multiplicity-corrected **p = 0.135**.

### Verdict — ⛔ **REFUTED**
The hour-of-day hole does not exist on independent data, and the *strongest* hour effect in
the fresh sample fails a 6-way permutation correction. Independently confirms §60's 3-bar
artifact call. **No hour gate, in either direction.** Close permanently — hour-of-day is a
dead axis for this fleet, third time measured.
