(1−a on DOWN, 1−b on UP): same fills, same counterparties, same cash (`a+b−1` = `1−(2−a−b)`). `mm.py`'s "D bid at

ask maker. Mint-specific constructions all measured: ask ladders + merge/skew −$2.2-3.2k/day (strat-ask-ladders);
mint as completing leg −3.25 ± 0.34 c/sh (strat-btc5m-scan §2); mint-then-sell = cash no-op / −3.8c per $1 both
legs (strat-mintsell); crossed-book cross 0.043 % of states, phantom; pair completion 11.1 % of bars at a sum of
1.037 (rebate-farm §4); gas ~0.02 c/sh. Only operational difference: a sold pair is cash now, no redemption wait.

## F. "Just hedge the maker inventory on Binance" — dead by arithmetic (recorded so it is not re-asked)
A near-the-money 5m binary with ~2.5 min left and ~7 bps of remaining σ has a delta of roughly φ(0)/σ ≈ **5-6 c of binary
price per 1 bp of spot**. Hedging ONE share therefore needs ≈ **$550-600 of perp notional** (a 50c share is ~1,000× levered
in bp terms). Binance taker 5 bps ⇒ **≈ $0.28 per share per round trip**, maker 2 bps ⇒ $0.11 — against a half-spread
capture of **0.87 c/share**. The hedge costs 13-30× the thing it protects. The same arithmetic explains WHY the book is so
adversely selected: a 1c binary tick ≈ 0.18 bps of BTC, ~20× the underlying's spread, so every resting quote is a free
option to anyone watching spot — and §9 of the rebate-farm doc measured that option's half-life at ~150 ms.
